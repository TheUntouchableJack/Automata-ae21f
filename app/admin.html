How each number is counted
  • Visitors: distinct visitor ids per day on the marketing pages, summed over the days. Marketing pages are cookieless, so one person is a new id every day: weekly or monthly uniques would read high, so none are shown.
  • Sign-up: an organization created. Staff invited to a team and customers of an app get no organization, so they don't count.
  • Confirmed email: the owner clicked the confirmation link.
  • Came back: the owner opened the dashboard more than 24 hours after signing up. Super Admin "view as" doesn't count.
  • Has a reward: at least one live reward. Publishing isn't used: every new app is published automatically.
  • First customer: at least one customer joined the business's app. The business's own team doesn't count.
  • Activated: at least 5 customers and at least one check-in by one of them.
  • Paid: a subscription that is active or trialing; its date is the last plan change. Churned: the subscription ended.
  • Est. MRR: each paid business at its tier's monthly list price (Starter $19.99, Growth $39.99, Scale $79.99; legacy Pro $299, Max $749). Annual vs monthly billing isn't stored.
  • Internal businesses (Royalty's own and test accounts) are left out unless "Include internal" is ticked. Mark one on the Organizations tab.
  • Days are UTC. A period of 30 days is today so far plus the 29 days before; "the days before" is the 30 days ahead of that.
