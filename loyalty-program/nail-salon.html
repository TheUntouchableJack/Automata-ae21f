Nail salon loyalty program ideas that keep clients on schedule

Nail clients come back every two to three weeks because their set grows out, about 20 visits a year. A program that rewards that rhythm works best: free nail art on the 4th visit, a gel upgrade on the 8th and a free manicure on the 12th, plus a reminder when someone is overdue for a fill.

Who this is for

This is for a nail salon, nail bar or studio doing manicures, gel and acrylic sets, fills and pedicures. The numbers assume a client who comes in every two to three weeks; pedicure-only clients who come monthly still reach the first rewards within a few months.

It helps most when clients book with whoever is free. A program that belongs to the salon gives them a reason to come back to you rather than the new place down the street.

Reward ideas, priced in visits

In Royalty, a nail salon program awards 10 points per visit, so 40 points means four visits.

  1. Free Nail Art 4 visits · 40 points

    Accent nails on their next set. It costs a few minutes and often turns into a paid add-on later.

  2. Free Paraffin Treatment 6 visits · 60 points

    Added to any manicure or pedicure. A treat that shows off a service many clients have never tried.

  3. Free Gel Upgrade 8 visits · 80 points

    Gel instead of regular polish at no extra cost, about four months in for a regular.

  4. Free Manicure 12 visits · 120 points, Regular tier and up

    The headline reward for clients who have stayed with you for half a year.

  5. Birthday polish change no points needed

    A free polish change in their birthday month, sent automatically.

The perks are suggestions. Pick the ones that cost you little and mean something to your regulars.

Four things that make it work

Put the QR code at the drying station

Clients have their phones out and nothing to do while their nails dry. A small stand with the code there turns waiting time into a check-in.

Remind clients when a fill is due

Two to three weeks after a gel or acrylic set, an automatic "time for your fill?" message gets the next appointment booked before the set lifts or chips.

Win back clients after 30 days

A regular who hasn’t been in for a month has usually tried somewhere else. A personal note with free nail art on their next visit is often enough to bring them back.

Send a birthday reward the week before

People book nails before a celebration. A birthday treat that arrives a week early fills the appointment they were already planning.

Questions owners ask

What are good loyalty rewards for a nail salon?

Rewards that cost you little time but feel like a treat: free nail art, a paraffin treatment, a gel upgrade, and a free manicure for long-time regulars. Price them in visits so a client who comes every two or three weeks reaches the first one within about two months.

How many visits before a free manicure?

Around 10 to 12 visits works for most salons. For a client on a two-to-three-week schedule that is roughly six months, long enough to be worth it for you and close enough that clients keep it in mind.

How do I get nail clients to rebook?

Rebook before they leave, and send an automatic reminder when they’re due for a fill. Showing them how close they are to their next reward at that moment makes the booking feel worth it.

Do clients need to download an app?

No. With Royalty they scan a QR code with their phone camera, which opens your loyalty app in the browser. They can add it to their home screen, but there is nothing to install.
